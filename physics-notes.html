1. Kinematics

Kinematics mein objects ki motion ko position, velocity aur acceleration ke through study kiya jata hai.

v = u + at
s = ut + ½at²
v² = u² + 2as
s = (u + v)t / 2

📝 Practice

Q1. Ek object rest se start karta hai aur acceleration 2 m/s² hai. 3 seconds baad velocity kya hogi?
Answer: 6 m/s
Q2. Agar u = 0, a = 2 m/s² aur t = 4 s ho, to displacement kya hoga?
Answer: 16 m

2. Laws of Motion

Newton ke laws force aur motion ke relation ko explain karte hain.

F = ma
Momentum: p = mv

Newton's third law ke according forces equal magnitude aur opposite direction mein act karte hain.

📝 Practice

Q1. 5 kg mass par 10 N force lagaya gaya. Acceleration kya hogi?
Answer: 2 m/s²
Q2. 4 kg object 3 m/s velocity se move kar raha hai. Momentum kya hoga?
Answer: 12 kg·m/s

3. Work, Energy & Power

Work tab hota hai jab force ke karan displacement hota hai.

W = Fs cosθ
Kinetic Energy = ½mv²
Potential Energy = mgh
Power = Work / Time

📝 Practice

Q1. 2 kg object ki velocity 3 m/s hai. Kinetic energy kya hogi?
Answer: 9 J
Q2. 100 J work 5 seconds mein kiya gaya. Power kya hogi?
Answer: 20 W

4. Gravitation

Newton ke law of gravitation ke according do masses ke beech gravitational force unke masses ke product ke proportional hota hai aur distance ke square ke inversely proportional hota hai.

F = Gm₁m₂ / r²
g = GM / R²

Earth ke near g ka approximate value 9.8 m/s² liya jata hai.

📝 Practice

Q1. Earth ke surface ke near gravitational acceleration ka approximate value kya hai?
Answer: 9.8 m/s²
Q2. Agar distance double ho jaye, gravitational force kitna ho jayega?
Answer: Original force ka 1/4

5. Current Electricity

Current electricity mein electric current, voltage aur resistance ke relations study kiye jate hain.

V = IR
I = Q / t
P = VI
P = I²R

📝 Practice

Q1. Resistance 5 Ω aur current 2 A hai. Voltage kya hoga?
Answer: 10 V
Q2. Voltage 12 V aur current 2 A hai. Power kya hogi?
Answer: 24 W
