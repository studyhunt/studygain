1. Quadratic Equations

Quadratic equation ka standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0, jahan a ≠ 0.

📌 Important Formulas

Discriminant: D = b² − 4ac
Roots: x = (−b ± √D) / 2a

📊 Nature of Roots

D > 0: Do distinct real roots

D = 0: Do equal real roots

D < 0: Real roots nahi hote

🎯 Useful Relations

α + β = −b/a
αβ = c/a

Yahan α aur β quadratic equation ke roots hain.

💡 JEE Tip

Question solve karte waqt sabse pehle equation ko standard form mein lao aur discriminant check karo. Conditions on roots wale questions mein α + β aur αβ ka use bahut useful hota hai.
