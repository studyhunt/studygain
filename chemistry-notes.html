1. Mole Concept

Mole concept chemistry ke numerical questions ka important foundation hai. 1 mole mein Avogadro number ke equal particles hote hain.

N = 6.022 × 10²³ particles/mol
Number of moles = Given mass / Molar mass
n = Number of particles / Nₐ

📝 Practice

Q1. 18 g water (H₂O) mein approximately kitne moles hain?
Answer: 1 mole
Q2. 1 mole substance mein approximately kitne particles hote hain?
Answer: 6.022 × 10²³

2. Atomic Structure

Atom ke basic particles electrons, protons aur neutrons hain. Atomic structure ko samajhna chemistry ke liye important hai.

Mass number = Protons + Neutrons
Atomic number = Number of protons

Neutral atom mein electrons ki sankhya protons ke barabar hoti hai.

📝 Practice

Q1. Kisi neutral atom ka atomic number 8 hai. Usmein electrons kitne honge?
Answer: 8 electrons
Q2. Agar kisi atom mein 11 protons aur 12 neutrons hain, to mass number kya hoga?
Answer: 23

3. Chemical Bonding

Chemical bonding atoms ke beech attractive interactions ko describe karti hai. Ionic aur covalent bonding important types hain.

Ionic bond → electron transfer
Covalent bond → electron sharing

Lewis structures aur octet rule bonding questions mein useful concepts hain.

📝 Practice

Q1. Ionic bonding mein generally kya hota hai?
Answer: Electrons ka transfer
Q2. Covalent bond mein atoms generally kya karte hain?
Answer: Electrons share karte hain

4. Thermodynamics

Thermodynamics mein heat, work aur energy changes ka study kiya jata hai.

ΔU = q + w
ΔH = H(products) − H(reactants)

Exothermic process mein heat release hoti hai, jabki endothermic process mein heat absorb hoti hai.

📝 Practice

Q1. Exothermic reaction mein heat release hoti hai ya absorb?
Answer: Release hoti hai
Q2. First law of thermodynamics ka common expression kya hai?
Answer: ΔU = q + w

5. Organic Chemistry Basics

Organic chemistry mainly carbon-containing compounds ka study hai. Functional groups ko identify karna bahut important hai.

Alcohol → −OH
Aldehyde → −CHO
Carboxylic acid → −COOH
Amine → −NH₂

📝 Practice

Q1. −OH functional group kis class of compounds mein common hai?
Answer: Alcohol
Q2. −COOH kis functional group ko represent karta hai?
Answer: Carboxylic acid
